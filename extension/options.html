SiteCleaner

The picker works on the tab you click it on, with no special permission. Access to every site is optional, and is what makes two things work:

  • Edits come back by themselves after a reload, and the picker stays on if it was on. Without it, you turn the picker on again and the edits are restored.
  • Screen-size previews, which open a second window and need to put your edits on it.
